## Purpose

Operational context of the current system.
Only includes behavior that is important for implementing features safely.

## App shell

* Rails **web** UI lives under the **`Web`** namespace (URLs prefixed with `/web/…` per `config/routes.rb`).
* **Devise**: sign-in only; **registrations are skipped** (`devise_for :users, skip: [:registrations]`).
* **Pundit** is included in `ApplicationController`; unauthorized access redirects with a flash.
* **Flash renders only from the layout:** `layouts/application.html.haml` shows `flash[:notice]` (success) and `flash[:alert]` (error) through `shared/_flash` above every signed-in page, for redirects and `flash.now` alike. Views must not render flash themselves (it showed twice). The one exception is `devise/sessions/new`: signed out, the layout skips flash, so the login view renders its own.
* **`User#role`**: `vendedor`, `caja`, `admin` (string-backed enum). Policies in `app/policies/` gate actions.
* **Turbo Streams exist in exactly one place: the cash day screen.** Every other form in the app submits in full and redirects. The only actions that answer `turbo_stream` are `Web::Cash::MovementsController#create/edit/update/destroy` and `Web::Cash::TransfersController#create` (templates `app/views/web/cash/{movements,transfers}/*.turbo_stream.haml`). Turbo Drive is active app-wide; that is unrelated.

## Web surface (what exists in routes)

* **Dashboard** (`web/dashboard#index`): metrics include “sales today” = sum of **active** (no canceladas) orders where **`created_at`** is today (not `sale_date`); receivables = sum of `Customer#current_balance` for customers with credit; low-stock lists; recent orders ordered by `created_at`. **Admin only**, an extra block "Caja de \<mes\>" (`_cash_month` partial) shows the month's sales and fixed expenses — gated on `policy(CashMovement).balance_report?`, and `Cash::Reports::MonthQuery` is not even instantiated for anyone else. The month travels as `?month=YYYY-MM`, is clamped to the current month (a future or unparseable value falls back to it), and the "next month" link is absent on the current month.
* **Text searches ignore accents and case**: `Product.search` (sku, name, brand), `Order.search_contact` (contact name) and the cash movement history's description search compare `unaccent(column) ILIKE unaccent(query)` (Postgres `unaccent` extension), so "bujia" finds "Bujías". Data is stored as typed; searches by number (paper, invoice, credit note) stay plain `ILIKE`. `Invoice.search_invoice` matches the number, the detail, the period as `MM/YYYY` or the whole reference as shown (`IVA · 09/2026`), with bound, `sanitize_sql_like`d params.
* **Products**: index/show/new/create + collection **`search`**. **`edit`/`update` implemented** (`admin` + `vendedor` via `ProductPolicy`; `caja` not). The `_form` posts to `create` or `update` depending on `product.persisted?`. Everything the form renders is editable **except `sku`** (readonly, OEM identity anchor) and **`current_stock`** (goes through movements). Changing `origin`/`product_type`/`brand` may collide with variant uniqueness (model validation) → error in the form. `price_unit` is the **counter/base price**; edited, it is the "next default" and the **write-back** of each sale can overwrite it (see "Manual pricing + write-back"). **Per-channel price:** `product_channel_prices` (product, channel, price > 0, unique per product + channel); `ProductChannelPrice::CHANNELS = %w[mercadolibre]` lists the channels with their own price. `Product#price_for(channel)` returns that row's price, else `price_unit` (nil or unknown channel → `price_unit`). Create/update go through `Products::Save` (one transaction; a blank channel price removes the row, an invalid one saves nothing). The form has "Precio Mercado Libre (ARS)" and the detail shows it or "Usa el de mostrador". `product-form#handleSubmit` (price_unit, cost_unit and the ML price) runs on `submit`, **not** `turbo:submit-start` — by then Turbo has built the body and "15.000" + Enter was read as 15. A refused save (422) echoes a positive ML price AR-formatted ("15.000,00") so resubmitting cannot inflate it; unparseable or ≤ 0 input is echoed as typed. Soft-deleting a product hard-deletes its channel prices (`dependent: :destroy`; there is no restore path).
* **Stock adjustments**: `resources :stock_adjustments` new/create → `Inventory::SetCountedStock`, admin only (`ProductPolicy#adjust_stock?`).
* **Orders**: index/show/new/create; member **POST `cancel`** → `Sales::CancelOrder`. Create builds items from **`purchase_items`** params and resolves customer via **`Customer.mostrador`** when `customer_id` is blank or `"mostrador"`. The `new` form shows only Customer · Type · Paper Number · Products (no "Discount" or "Payment Detail"). Submit enables with items + customer + paper_number. **`#index` is the global sales history**, filtered by Tipo · Estado · Período · búsqueda por N° de nota, paginated with **pagy**. The **status filter selects the date axis**: `settled_on` when Estado = Cobradas, `sale_date` otherwise. **Search mode** (paper number present) **ignores every other filter** (type/status/period).
* **Customers**: index/show/new/create/edit/update; **`debtors`** collection → list of customers with balance > 0 ordered by debt; nested **`payments`** new/create → `Payments::AllocatePayment` (module `Web::Customers`). The collection form lets you distribute the payment across one or several pending orders with an independent payment method per row.
* **Sale notes** (caja): index + cancel + nested payment new/create. `Web::SaleNotesController#index` lists `Order.immediate.pending`; `Web::SaleNotes::PaymentsController` collects via `Payments::CollectSaleNote` (global discount 0/5/10, multi-tender, cash-only rule). Sidebar entry "Notas de pedido" visible to caja+admin with a pending-count badge.
* **Payments on account** (pago a cuenta): `resources :payments_on_account` index/show + member **POST `deliver`** + nested payment new/create. `Web::PaymentsOnAccountController#index` lists `Order.open_on_account` (not settled) with search by contact (`search_contact`); `#show` is the vendor's view (marks deliveries); `#deliver` → `Inventory::MarkDelivered`. `Web::PaymentsOnAccount::PaymentsController` collects via `Payments::CollectOnAccount`. Roles via `PaymentOnAccountPolicy`: view list/detail = vendedor+caja+admin, mark delivery = vendedor+admin, collect = caja+admin (in `show` the controls are gated with `@can_deliver` / `@can_collect`). Sidebar entry "Pagos a cuenta" visible to all three roles with a badge of open operations. The `show` summary reads *Total de la venta* (`original_total_amount`) · *Descuentos* (`Order#discounts_total` = original − `total_amount`, hidden at 0) · *Pagado* · *Saldo*, and its history lists each allocation's *Recibido* and *Bajó la deuda* (received + `discount_amount`, with " · N%" from `PaymentsHelper#payment_allocation_discount_percent`). The index list still shows `total_amount` as "Total".
* **Payment page** (`resources :payments, only: [:show, :update]`, `PaymentPolicy`: caja + admin): one collection (`Payment`) — the notes it covers, its cash movements and its invoice. `update` records or corrects the invoice through `Payments::AssignInvoice`; `?facturar=1` renders the invoice form already open. Reached from the cash day rows and from the closing modal's unbilled list (see "Caja").
* **Suppliers**: full `resources` (includes destroy). The form (new/edit) has a **"Qué factura"** block (`_expense_types` partial, `suppliers.expense_types`) and a `_payment_details` partial where bank details and payment terms sit **collapsed behind "+ Agregar …" links**, offered only when the supplier bills Proveedor or Servicios (`Supplier#offers_payment_terms?`; `supplier_form_controller.js` toggles, the initial state is rendered server-side). On edit a bank card that already holds values stays usable whatever the types, so stored details can still be changed or removed. `Web::SuppliersController#supplier_params` **nils the three payment-term keys** when the posted types are non-empty and include neither `supplier` nor `utilities` (the disabled inputs are not posted, so stale terms would otherwise block the save); an all-unchecked submit is refused without touching them.
* **Invoices**: simple-mode UI only (see below): index/new/create/show/edit/update; **`pending`** list; **`mark_supplier_paid`**; member **`mark_as_paid`**, **`cancel`** (pending invoice → `cancelled` via `Invoices::CancelInvoice`). Both pay actions read `account` + `payment_date` and go through `Invoices::PayInvoices` (see "Invoices"). **`#index`** is filtered by Proveedor · Tipo · Estado (Pendientes default · Pagadas · Canceladas) · búsqueda (number, detail, period or reference), paginated with **pagy**, with a *Tipo* column and a *Comprobante* column showing `Invoice#reference`. The three metric cards (deuda pendiente, crédito, balance neto) stay independent of the status filter — they honour supplier, type and search; with a non-supplier type the credit cards read `CreditNote.none` (credit notes only apply to supplier invoices).

The supplier select lists only `Supplier.billing("supplier")` (plus the note's current supplier on edit), and `CreditNote` refuses a supplier that does not bill Proveedor (only on create or supplier change). **`#index`** is filtered by Proveedor · Estado (Todos · Disponibles · Aplicadas · Canceladas) · búsqueda por N° de nota, paginated with **pagy**. * **Caja** (`namespace :cash`, everything under `/web/cash/…`, helpers `web_cash_*`): `days` **index/show only**, with **`param: :business_date`** (the segment is a date string, `Date.parse`d by hand; `#index` only redirects to today). Two report screens, both GET: `reports/balance` (`web_cash_balance_report_path`) and `reports/history` (`web_cash_movement_history_path`). The five movement/transfer actions answer **turbo_stream**; the day, the closing and both reports answer HTML. **The whole module is admin-only today** (see Caja below).

## Core flows

### Orders

* `order_type` enum: `immediate`, `credit` and `on_account` (see "Payments on account" below). `status` enum: `pending`, `confirmed`, `cancelled` (default `pending`). Orders are born `pending`; they promote to `confirmed` automatically when `outstanding_balance == 0` via `Order#refresh_status_from_balance!` (called from `Payments::AllocatePayment`, `Payments::CollectSaleNote` and `Payments::CollectOnAccount` at the end of the transaction).
* Created via `Sales::CreateOrder` (from `Web::OrdersController#create`). It receives `customer:, items:, order_type:, paper_number:, user:, channel:, source:, sale_date:, contact_name:, contact_phone:, delivered_product_ids:`. **It captures neither payments nor discount** — the vendor generates a note; caja collects it later (immediate) or it enters accounts receivable (credit).
* **The goods leave the shelf with the note.** `Sales::CreateOrder` writes one `sale` `StockMovement` per line (referencing the `OrderItem`) for `immediate` and `credit` orders, and for the `on_account` lines in `delivered_product_ids`, through `Inventory::DeductLineStock` — **floored at zero**: a line takes `min(quantity, current_stock)`, writes nothing when that is 0, and **a sale is never refused for stock** (the old `source: "live"` block is gone). `source` no longer gates stock and the form always submits `from_paper`, but the column is still read by `Order`'s `total_amount` rule and by `Sales::ReplaceOrderItem`'s zero-total guard (see Key constraints).
* `paper_number` is **required for every note** (live and from_paper).
* The `new` form shows Customer · Type · Paper Number · Products (no "Discount" or "Payment Detail"). Each line has an **editable unit price** (`currency-input` input, AR format `1.500.000,50`, prefilled with the selected channel's price, see below). Submit enables with items + customer + paper_number + **all prices > 0**.
* **Manual pricing + write-back:** `Sales::CreateOrder` requires `unit_price > 0` for **every** item, in **all** `source` values (it no longer tolerates nil/0 — the old `from_paper` mode with a null price no longer exists). When creating the order, it remembers each typed price within the same transaction (`Sales::CreateOrder#remember_price`): a channel with its own price (`ProductChannelPrice.own_price?`) upserts that channel's row and **never touches `price_unit`**; counter, WhatsApp and nil keep writing `product.price_unit`. `price_unit` is not protected like stock/cost, so a direct `update!` is the correct mechanism.
* **New-sale form and channel price:** lines prefill with the selected channel's price (`Product#channel_prices_map` in the search JSON; `initial_items` carry `channel_prices` + `base_price`). Changing the channel re-prefills **every** line, typed prices included, and shows "Se actualizaron los precios al canal X." (the notice hides when the channel changes with no lines); an own-price channel also shows a help line saying the typed price updates that channel's price, not the counter's. The server-side preloads (Vender / cart) use the counter price. **Known gap (pre-existing):** a failed order create re-renders an empty new-sale form with the channel back on Mostrador.
* Cancelled via `Sales::CancelOrder` (member cancel). `OrderPolicy#cancel_pending?` allows vendedor/caja/admin on `pending` notes; `#cancel?` only admin on `confirmed`. The controller picks the policy method based on the state.
* `settled_on` (date column) stores the **`MAX(payments.payment_date)`** across the order's allocations. `Order#refresh_status_from_balance!` stamps it when the balance reaches 0 and clears it if the order reopens; `Sales::CancelOrder` also clears it on cancellation. It is a **`date`**, not a `datetime`, so ties within a day are the norm — every ordering that uses it (or `sale_date`) requires the **`id` tiebreaker**.

#### Local vs imported price (decision 2026-06-26)

* **Resolution of the problem "the same part has two prices depending on the batch's origin".** The per-origin price list (a single product with local/imported rates) and all dual-price variants were **discarded**. **There is no selectable local/imported rate at collection time nor costs separated by origin** — the fine-grained improvement is left for later.
* **Adopted mechanism (already covered by existing behavior):**
  1. **The price is set manually on each sale** — editable per-line price in `web/orders/new` + **write-back** to `product.price_unit` (or the channel's own price for Mercado Libre; see "Manual pricing + write-back" above). The catalog price adjusts with each sale; that is **intentional**, not a bug.
  2. **The price can be edited** from the product screen (`web/products/:id/edit`, admin+vendedor), but that edit sets the "next default" — the **write-back** of the next sale can overwrite it. `current_stock` still cannot be edited (movements); `cost_unit` is editable but `recalculate_average_cost!` recomputes it when purchases are confirmed.
  3. **Origin change** (e.g. imported USA sold out → local/china repurchase): **best case**, a **new product** is created with that `origin` (variant by `sku + product_type + origin + brand`); **worst case**, the next sale price simply overwrites the existing product's `price_unit` via write-back.
* **Honest assumed limitation:** `cost_unit` is still an average that mixes local and imported purchases, so the margin by origin is not exact. It does not get worse than today.
* **Selling moves stock** — `Sales::CreateOrder` writes one floored `sale` movement per line and `Sales::CancelOrder` puts back each line's net (see "Orders" and "Stock"). Stock never refuses a sale, so a product with nothing left can still be sold; it just moves nothing.
* Immediate discount: caja applies it at collection time via `Payments::CollectSaleNote`. Cap `0 / 5 / 10` (global), distributed across all `order_items`. **Rule:** only allowed if the whole amount is paid in cash (validated in the backend and enforced in Stimulus). **Exact totals (rounding down only under a discount, see below):** `effective_total = (original_total × (1 − desc/100)).round(2)`; the tenders must cover it (0.01 tolerance only on the short side). **Rounding down to the hundred (fix_18):** with a discount, the tenders may fall short of `effective_total` down to `minimum_charge` = `effective_total` floored to the hundred (when that floor is positive); the note then closes at what was charged (`total_amount = tender_sum`), and the shortfall shows as the order's "Redondeo" line. Without a discount the exact total is still required. The form mirrors it: within that range the "Por pagar" row reads "Redondeo −$ X" and submit is enabled; below it a gray hint reads "Con descuento se puede redondear hasta $ X." `order_item.discount_percent` is written as display metadata. **Cash may exceed the total:** any positive excess is *overpaid*; it is allowed only if it fits inside the cash tenders (otherwise "Lo cobrado de más solo puede ser en efectivo"). The excess is added to `total_amount` (`effective_total + overpaid`) and recorded in the cash allocation's `overpaid_amount`. The form (`sale_note_payment_controller`) mirrors this: it shows "Cobrado de más" in the summary and asks `window.confirm` before submit. **The server only books the overpayment the operator confirmed:** the form sends the excess it showed in the hidden `confirmed_overpaid` field (`0` when there is none, parsed like the tender amounts); when the computed overpaid is positive and differs from it by more than 0.01, the service refuses with "El saldo cambió mientras cobrabas. Revisá el monto." (a computed overpaid of 0 ignores the field). The form computes the discounted total in integer cents, rounding half-up like the server (1.234,50 at 5% → 1.172,78). `Order#original_total_at_least_current_total` validates `on: :create` only, because an overpayment raises `total_amount` above `original_total_amount` on update.
* **Discount display (`web/orders/show`):** discount, overpayment and (legacy) rounding are separate lines. `Order#discount_amount` is the **nominal** discount (sum of `qty × unit × discount_percent/100` per item), **not** `original − total`. `Order#overpaid_amount` = `payment_allocations.sum(:overpaid_amount)` (cash collected above what was owed; part of `total_amount`). `Order#discounts_total` = `original_total_amount − total_amount + overpaid_amount` (on-account `show` summary, hidden at 0). `Order#rounding_amount` = `total_amount − (original_total_amount − discount_amount) − overpaid_amount`; it is non-zero on legacy orders rounded by a removed cash-rounding rule and on sale notes charged rounded down to the hundred under a cash discount (fix_18, negative); both show a "Redondeo" line. Detail shows a "Cobrado de más" line (`+overpaid`) when positive; the right-hand summary's no-discount branch shows Subtotal = `total_amount − overpaid_amount`. Reconciles: `Subtotal − Discount (+ Redondeo) + Cobrado de más = Total`. **Accepted gap:** cancelling an overpaid order destroys its allocations, so `overpaid_amount` drops to 0 while `total_amount` keeps the excess; `orders/show` then shows that difference as a legacy "Redondeo" line. Cancelled orders are out of caja and the dashboard, so nothing else reads it.
* Credit discount: unchanged with respect to feat_09 (per-item 0-20% on the first collection via `Payments::AllocatePayment`; frozen after the first allocation).

### Payments on account (`on_account`)

* Third `order_type`: **`on_account`** — a counter sale that is paid in installments and delivered progressively. Born `pending`; promotes to `confirmed` when `outstanding_balance == 0`.
* **Contact required** (this type only): `contact_name` / `contact_phone` columns in `orders`, validated in `Order#on_account_requires_contact` and in `Sales::CreateOrder`. The associated customer stays as Mostrador; it does **not** enter `Customer#current_balance` (the formula only counts `credit`).
* **Per-item delivery is the stock event.** `order_items.delivered_at` (nullable) is set by the **vendedor**, not caja: at creation (`Sales::CreateOrder` with `delivered_product_ids`) or later from the detail view via `Inventory::MarkDelivered` (member `POST deliver`). Marking a line delivered takes it off the shelf (`Inventory::DeductLineStock`, floored at zero, note `"Entrega nota N"`); `delivered: false` (service only, no screen) puts back the line's net (`Inventory::RestoreLineStock`). The service is per line, idempotent and transactional — a line already in the asked state is left alone — and it refuses a cancelled order (`PaymentOnAccountPolicy#deliver?` hides the button there too), since the cancellation could not give that stock back. **`Sales::ReplaceOrderItem` moves no stock**: it only touches undelivered lines, which have taken nothing.
* **Per-item product swap (feat_22)**: vendedor/admin can replace the product and/or quantity of an **undelivered** line from the detail view (`Web::PaymentsOnAccount::ItemsController` edit/update → `Sales::ReplaceOrderItem`). The new line price is the chosen product's `price_for(order.channel)` (its channel price, else `price_unit`; rejected if not > 0; `item_swap_controller` shows the same via the `channel` value); a quantity-only edit keeps the line's original price. The delta is applied to **both** `total_amount` and `original_total_amount` (never re-summed — that would wipe effective discounts), then `refresh_status_from_balance!` runs (a pricier swap can reopen a confirmed order). No stock movements. **Known gap (accepted):** there is no overpayment guard — a swap below the collected amount leaves a negative `outstanding_balance` on screen and auto-confirms the order; the excess is not recorded anywhere. After such a swap, the order's existing `payment_allocations` exceed the reduced `total_amount` and would fail `PaymentAllocation#amount_within_order_outstanding_balance` if ever re-saved — nothing re-saves them today (`Sales::CancelOrder` uses `destroy_all`, which skips validation; `Payments::CollectOnAccount` refuses any collection that would lower the debt past the balance), so the risk is latent, not active.
* **Settled** (leaves the open list) only with `outstanding_balance == 0` **and** all items with `delivered_at` (`Order#settled?` / `#fully_delivered?`). Scope `Order.open_on_account` (Postgres `HAVING`: balance > 0 **OR** undelivered items). Search by contact: `Order.search_contact`.
* **Repeatable partial collection** via `Payments::CollectOnAccount` (sibling of `CollectSaleNote`). It receives `order:, tenders:, discount_percent:` — **no `amount_to_settle`**: caja enters what the customer hands over (the tenders) and the service derives `settled`, how far that lowers the debt. Without a discount, `settled` = what was received. With a per-event discount `0/5/10` (**cash only**), `settled` = received / (1 − d) **rounded to the peso** (800.000 at 10% → 888.889). The cash that settles the whole balance is `settle_all_cash = (balance × (1 − d)).round(2)` (exact, no cap; with a discount, cash down to its hundred floor also settles — see `rounded_down?`). The whole balance is settled only when what is received equals `settle_all_cash` **exactly** (an exact transfer at 0% also settles), there is cash *overpaid*, or — with a discount — the cash is at least `settle_all_cash` floored to the hundred (`rounded_down?`, fix_18; the shortfall joins the allocation's `discount_amount`); otherwise there is no short-side tolerance (a transfer 1 cent short is a partial collection). The form shows a "Redondeo" row with the shortfall and "Queda debiendo $ 0" in that range. **Overpaid** = `received − settle_all_cash` when positive and covered by the cash tenders; it is added to the total: `apply_totals!` sets `total_amount = total_amount − discount + overpaid`, where `discount = settled − received + overpaid`. A transfer above the balance is refused with "Es más de lo que debe. Para saldar todo [con N%] corresponde cobrar $ X" (X = `settle_all_cash`, AR format). **Guards against a duplicate or stale submit:** an operation with `outstanding_balance <= 0` is refused ("La operación ya está saldada"), and a positive overpaid must match the form's `confirmed_overpaid` within 0.01 (else "El saldo cambió mientras cobrabas. Revisá el monto."). `original_total_amount` is untouched. Each `PaymentAllocation` records the cash received; the cash one carries `discount_amount` and `overpaid_amount` (0 otherwise; collections from before fix_14 have 0 discount, before fix_15 0 overpaid). It calls `refresh_status_from_balance!` at the end. The form (`on_account_payment_controller`) mirrors the math, shows an "overpaid" row and confirms before submit ("Vas a cobrar $ X de más en efectivo"). On the `show` page, *Bajó la deuda* = amount + `discount_amount` − `overpaid_amount`, and the summary lists "Cobrado de más" when positive.
* Collection screen: caja fills **"¿Cuánto recibís?"** — `tenders[N][payment_method]` / `tenders[N][amount]` rows (same multi-tender shape as the sale-note form), always editable and **empty on load** — plus `discount_percent`. A **"Saldar todo: cobrar $ X"** button fills the settle-all cash for the chosen discount. `on_account_payment_controller` mirrors the service rule to show *Debe hoy · Recibís · Descuento N% en efectivo · Redondeo · Baja la deuda en · Queda debiendo · Cobrado de más*; past the balance with an excess the cash does not cover, it shows the same excess message and disables submit, while cash above the settle-all amount shows "Cobrado de más" and asks to confirm; on submit it writes the excess it showed into `confirmed_overpaid`. "Saldar todo" computes in integer cents, rounding half-up like the server. A non-cash row disables the discount select. The service has the last word; a refused POST re-renders (422) with the error but **without the typed amounts**. Delivery visible **read-only** on this screen.

### Stock

* Persisted movements are always **`StockMovement`** rows (product + **required** `stock_location`).
* **`Product#current_stock`** is refreshed by **`#recalculate_current_stock!`** (sum of movement quantities); **there are no `StockMovement` model callbacks** that update the product — callers/services do it after writes (e.g. `Inventory::AdjustStock`).
* UI and sales code paths use **`StockLocation.first!`** when a location is needed.
* **Invoices with lines write `purchase` movements at registration and floored `adjustment` reversals on cancel** (`Invoices::CreateInvoice` / `CancelInvoice`). **Sales write `sale` movements that reference the `OrderItem`** — at creation for immediate/credit, at delivery for on_account — and `Sales::CancelOrder` puts back each line's **net** (`-order_item.stock_movements.sum(:quantity)`) as an `adjustment`, in the same transaction as the cash reversal. `Order#sale_movements` gathers a sale's rows through its lines; `Order has_many :stock_movements, as: :reference` is no longer written.
* **Admins can set a product's stock to a counted number** from `/web/stock_adjustments/new` ("Ajuste de stock" in the sidebar, or "Ajustar stock" on the product page), several products per save. `Inventory::SetCountedStock` takes the difference at save time, under the product's row lock, against the sum of the product's movements (not the cached `current_stock`, so a drifted cache still lands on the count), and writes one `adjustment` movement per changed product through `Inventory::AdjustStock`, carrying the admin (`stock_movements.user_id`, null for every other movement) and the required reason as `note`. All or nothing; a batch with no change is refused. Gated by `ProductPolicy#adjust_stock?`. The old nested `products/:id/stock_movements` page is gone.

### Invoices (simple mode in the UI)

* **`Invoices::CreateInvoice`** (was `CreateSimpleInvoice`) creates **`has_items: false`**, **`status: pending`** records — with or without **product lines** (`items: [{product_id, quantity, unit_cost}]`). A line counts only with a product and a quantity > 0; a blank unit cost is 0, an unparseable one is refused. With lines, `amount = Σ(quantity × unit_cost)` is computed server-side (the submitted amount is ignored), the `invoice_items` are **built before `save!`** so the model's amount guard sees them, and one **`purchase` `StockMovement`** per line (`reference: invoice`, `StockLocation.first!`) is written through `Inventory::AdjustStock` — all in one transaction. `has_items` stays false on purpose: `Invoice#total_amount`, `simple_mode`, `mark_as_paid?`, the early-payment discount and credit notes all read `amount` and never notice the lines. `Invoice`'s `amount > 0` rule applies only when there are no items (`amount_required?`); an itemized invoice may sum to 0.
* **Canceling** goes through **`Invoices::CancelInvoice`**: it reverses the invoice's actual `purchase` movements per product as one `adjustment`, **floored at the product's `current_stock`** (a product with nothing left gets no row; the cancellation always succeeds), then sets `status: cancelled`. An amount-only invoice has no purchase rows and only changes status. `InvoicePolicy#cancel?` still requires `pending`.
* **Editing an itemized invoice never touches the lines** and ignores `amount` (`Web::InvoicesController#update` drops it; the edit view renders it read-only). Every other header field, `exchange_rate` included, stays editable.
* **The form** (`new.html.haml`): a Productos card driven by `invoice-lines` (Stimulus) over the reused `product-search` (with `dimOutOfStock: false`); the amount freezes to the sum while complete lines exist; a submit with a zero-cost line opens a confirmation modal first (and only then); a failed submit re-renders with the header from `params` and the lines from `@submitted_items`. Lines post as `items[N][…]`.
* **Every invoice has a type** (`invoices.expense_type`, `Invoice::EXPENSE_TYPE_LABELS`: `supplier` Proveedor · `taxes` Impuestos · `utilities` Servicios · `social_charges` Cargas sociales; default `supplier`). The type decides the cash category of its payment (`Invoice#cash_category_attrs`: `suppliers`, or `fixed_expense` + the same-named subcategory). Rent, salaries and store expenses are not invoices: they stay hand-typed cash outflows.
  * **Only `supplier` invoices carry product lines** (model validation; on create the products card is hidden and cleared for other types, and on edit the type is read-only once lines exist — to change it, cancel the invoice, which returns the stock, and register it again).
  * **Identification differs by type.** A supplier invoice needs `invoice_number`. Taxes / utilities / social charges have **no number**: they need `invoices.period` (stored as the 1st of the month; the form's month input defaults to the previous month and `parse_period` also accepts `MM/YYYY`) and may carry `invoices.detail` (≤ 40 chars, e.g. "IVA", "SICOSS"); they are **ARS only** ("Las boletas de <Tipo> son en pesos"). `Invoice#reference` is the operator-facing identity everywhere — the number for a supplier, `"IVA · 09/2026"` / `"09/2026"` otherwise — and `#reference_was` (persisted values) titles the edit page. `before_save :clear_unused_fields` drops what the type does not use (number, or period/detail, ARS exchange rate) only once a save goes through, so a refused edit re-renders with what was typed.
  * **A supplier declares what it bills** (`suppliers.expense_types`, string array, default `["supplier"]`; `Supplier.billing(type)`, `#bills?`). The new/edit invoice forms list only suppliers billing the chosen type (options carry `data-expense-types`; on edit the saved supplier is kept under the saved type), and `Invoice` refuses "<proveedor> no factura <Tipo>" — **only on create or when supplier/type change**, so an old invoice stays payable if its supplier later drops the type.
  * **Early-payment terms apply to supplier and utilities invoices only** (`Invoice#early_payment_applicable?`): `set_early_payment_terms` copies the supplier's terms only for those types, and `clear_unused_fields` drops them on taxes / social charges (including a type change on edit). `Supplier` likewise refuses payment terms unless it bills Proveedor or Servicios.
* **Paying an invoice takes money out of an arca.** **`Invoices::PayInvoices.call(invoices:, account:, payment_date:, user:, credit_note_ids: [])`** is the **only writer** of "paid" (it replaced `MarkAsPaid` / `ProcessPayment`). In one transaction it locks the invoices (`FOR UPDATE`, by id) and then the credit notes, validates, applies credits, writes **one** outflow through `Cash::RecordMovement` and marks every invoice paid pointing at it (`invoices.cash_movement_id`; `CashMovement has_many :paid_invoices`):
  * **`account ∈ ACCOUNTS = drawer main_cash bank mercado_pago`** ("Elegí de dónde sale la plata"; never Remanente or USD). A drawer payment lowers *Monto a fajar*; no payment touches *Ventas del día*.
  * **`payment_date`** not future, not before any invoice date, and **not a closed day** ("El día dd/mm/aaaa está cerrado. Usá una fecha abierta.").
  * **One supplier and one type per batch** ("Pagá por separado las facturas de distinto tipo"); credit notes only on supplier-type invoices. A double submit waits on the lock and then fails "ya está pagada".
  * **Net in pesos:** Σ `Invoice#amount_due_ars(payment_date)` (USD × the invoice's rate; the early-payment discount only up to its deadline, decided by the **payment** date) minus the credits applied. Credits are capped per invoice at what it still owes **in pesos**; a USD note's application is stored in the note's currency (`AppliedCredit.amount`, rounded up with `ROUND_CEILING` so a covering note leaves no residue). Net 0 → invoices paid, **no outflow**.
  * The outflow's description is `"Pago <proveedor> — <references>"`; `Invoice#paid_with_credits_only?` lets the invoice page tell "no salió plata" apart from legacy invoices paid before outflows existed.
* **Pagos pendientes** (`pending`) groups by **supplier + type**, so AFIP shows once per type and a batch never mixes types; per-invoice amounts are `amount_due_ars` and the header cards are summed from the groups. Its batch modal (inline JS in `pending.html.haml`) adds *Sale de* (confirm disabled until chosen), recomputes amounts when the payment date moves into or out of an early-payment window, and escapes every interpolated value (`escapeHtml`). The invoice page's *Pagar* modal (`_pay_modal`, `invoice-payment` Stimulus) does the same for one invoice; a paid invoice shows "Pagada el … desde <arca>" with a link to that cash day.
* **Credit-note availability** = `CreditNote#available?` (`active_status? && !exhausted?`), **not** the `available`/`where(status: "active")` scope. The `status` enum is only `active`/`cancelled`; a fully-applied note stays `active` but is **exhausted** (`remaining_balance <= 0`). For *count* metrics use `.count(&:available?)` so exhausted notes are excluded (they already contribute 0 to amount sums via `remaining_balance`). Applies to invoices index (`@credit_notes_count`), credit-notes index, and `Supplier#credit_notes_count`. **`CreditNote.with_balance` / `.exhausted_credits`** (correlated SQL subquery on `applied_credits`, used by `by_availability` to filter/paginate the credit-notes index) are the **SQL twin of `available?`** — the same "active and has balance" rule expressed once in Ruby and once in SQL. Keep both in sync when the rule changes.

### Customer account payments (`Payment` + `PaymentAllocation` models)

* **`Payment`** represents a *tender* (a physical delivery of money with a single method). It belongs to the `customer`; **it no longer has `order_id`**. Validations: `amount > 0`, `payment_method ∈ Payment::PAYMENT_METHODS` (single source: `PAYMENT_METHOD_LABELS` in `app/models/payment.rb` → `cash bank_qr bank_card bank_transfer mercado_pago`; labels via `Payment.method_label` / UI options via `Payment.method_options`), `payment_date` present. **It no longer requires `has_credit_account?`** — Payments can belong to retail (mostrador) customers. It also carries its own **invoice** (`invoice_type`, `invoice_number`; see "Caja").
* **The payment method is always picked explicitly** on the three collection forms (sale note `sale_notes/payments/new`, on-account `payments_on_account/payments/new`, credit `customers/payments/new`). Each method select renders through `PaymentsHelper#payment_method_options_with_prompt`: a disabled, preselected **"Seleccionar medio"** option (value `""`, so the field is not submitted) followed by `Payment.method_options` — nothing defaults to cash. Rows added with "+ Agregar método" reset to the prompt. **Front:** the submit stays disabled, with the hint "Falta seleccionar el medio de pago", while a row with amount > 0 (or, on the credit form, a ticked order with an amount) has no method; a row without a method does **not** count as non-cash for the cash-only discount rule. **Back:** `CollectSaleNote`, `CollectOnAccount` and `AllocatePayment` refuse a blank method before the "Método de pago inválido" check with `Payment::MISSING_METHOD_ERROR` ("No se puede guardar el cobro sin medio de pago"; `AllocatePayment` appends "(orden #N)"); the controllers re-render 422 with it. **Enter never submits these forms:** the generic `enter-blur` Stimulus controller (`keydown->enter-blur#blur` on the form) cancels Enter in any input and blurs it, so `currency-input` formats the amount and the summary recalculates; the form submits only from its button.
* **Changing a collection's payment method (feat_38):** on `web/payments/show`, the "Medio de pago" card (`_payment_method`, opened/closed by the `payment-invoice` Stimulus controller) lets caja/admin (`PaymentPolicy#change_method?`) `PATCH /web/payments/:id/payment_method` (`Web::PaymentsController#change_method`). `Payments::ChangeMethod` corrects **in place**, inside a lock on the payment and its movement: `payment.payment_method` and the original cash movement's `channel`/`account` (via `PAYMENT_METHOD_CHANNELS` / `CHANNEL_ACCOUNTS`); amount, date, collector and invoice stay. `Payment#method_change_block` is the single rule shared by view and service: `:locked` (no movement or reversed), `:closed` (a `DailyClosing` for the movement's date, or the movement sealed), `:cash_discount` (cash with `Payment#cash_discounted?`: an allocation's `discount_amount`/`overpaid_amount`, or an immediate note with a discount; credit item discounts are not cash-only and don't block). The service also refuses a blank, unknown or unchanged method. The cash day reads live, so its rows and per-channel totals follow. **Accepted gap:** a change committed while `Cash::CloseDay` runs on the same date can land after the closing read the expected cash.
* **`PaymentAllocation`** (join `payment_allocations(payment_id, order_id, amount, discount_amount, overpaid_amount)`) distributes a Payment's amount across one or several orders. `discount_amount` (default 0) is only written by `Payments::CollectOnAccount`: the cash discount that collection added on top of the cash to lower the debt. `overpaid_amount` (default 0, NOT NULL) is the part of the allocation's `amount` that exceeded what the order owed — only ever on a cash allocation, written by `CollectSaleNote`, `CollectOnAccount` and `AllocatePayment`; it is already part of the order's `total_amount`. Validations: `amount > 0`, `order_belongs_to_payment_customer`, `amount_within_order_outstanding_balance` (with `where.not(id: id)` to exclude the current allocation; the overpayment is added to `total_amount` *before* the allocation is created, so it passes). Unique index `(payment_id, order_id)` — because of it, `Payments::CollectSaleNote` / `CollectOnAccount` group tenders by method and write **one** allocation per method: repeated rows of the same method are summed, not inserted twice (inserting twice raised `RecordNotUnique`, surfaced as the generic "Error registrando el cobro").
* **Invariant:** `payment.amount == SUM(payment.allocations.amount)` — guaranteed by the service, not by a model validation.
* **`Payments::AllocatePayment`** service for credit collection. It receives `customer:, payment_date:, notes:, allocations: [{order_id:, amount:, payment_method:, item_discounts: {oi_id => percent}}]`. It accepts `pending` or `confirmed` orders (rejects only `cancelled`). It groups by `payment_method`, creates one `Payment` per group + its `PaymentAllocation`s, and calls `refresh_status_from_balance!` on each touched order at the end of the transaction. **Item discounts are exact:** `apply_discounts_for` writes the exact discounted sum (per-line `round(2)`) into `total_amount`; there is no `canonical_total` and no rounding. **Overpaid cash:** `settle_overpayment!` runs per row after the discounts; if `amount` exceeds the order's `outstanding_balance` and the row is `cash`, `total_amount` is raised by the excess and the row's `overpaid_amount` is recorded; a non-cash row above the outstanding is refused ("Lo cobrado de más solo puede ser en efectivo (orden #N, saldo $ X)"). **Guards against a duplicate or stale submit:** a row on an order with `outstanding_balance <= 0` is refused ("La orden #N ya está saldada"), and a positive overpaid must match the row's `confirmed_overpaid` within 0.01 (else "El saldo cambió mientras cobrabas. Revisá el monto."). A partial credit payment stays free-form. The form (`payment_allocation_controller`) rounds per-line amounts to cents half-up before summing (as the server does), shows "Cobrado de más" per row and confirms before submit, writing each card's shown excess into `allocations[i][confirmed_overpaid]`; changing the payment method only refreshes the summary, it no longer re-prefills the amount.
* **`Sales::CancelOrder`** takes a required **`user:`** (the cash reversal needs an author). It **reverses the cash before destroying the allocations** — the allocations are the only path back to the payments — and then destroys the `PaymentAllocation`s of the cancelled order (`@order.payment_allocations.destroy_all`).
  * It reverses (via `Cash::ReversePayment`) only the payments the order owns **outright** — those with no allocation to any other order — and only if they already have a cash movement (payments predating the cash module write nothing). Any failure raises and rolls the whole cancellation back.
  * The `Payment`s **stay alive on purpose**: `cash_movements.source_payment_id` has an FK to them and the reversal row means nothing without the payment it names. It is no longer harmful — `Customer#current_balance` only counts allocations of non-cancelled orders, so a payment left with no allocation weighs on no balance.
  * **Known gap (accepted):** a payment split across several orders where only one is cancelled is **not** reversed at all — `Cash::ReversePayment` mirrors a movement whole and the design has no partial reversal (`docs/CASH_MODULE_DESIGN.md` §4.2). The money stays in the arca (it never physically went back out), but the cancelled order's slice becomes unallocated customer surplus with no record of it.
* `Order#outstanding_balance` = `total_amount − payment_allocations.sum(:amount)` for non-cancelled orders (any type). Automatic promotion to `confirmed` when it reaches 0 via `Order#refresh_status_from_balance!`, invoked from `Payments::AllocatePayment` and `Payments::CollectSaleNote` at the end of the transaction.
* `Customer#current_balance` = `SUM(credit_orders.total_amount) − SUM(payment_allocations on credit orders)` filtering `status IN ('pending', 'confirmed')`. `Customer.with_outstanding_balance` uses the same formula. `on_account` (payment on account) orders do **not** enter: the formula only counts `order_type: 'credit'`.
* **`Customer#last_payment_date`** / **`#days_without_paying`** — helpers for the debtors view.
* **`Customer.with_outstanding_balance`** scope — customers whose confirmed credit sales exceed the total of their payments.
* **`Web::Customers::PaymentsController#new`** shows a table with all pending orders; each row has an inclusion checkbox, an editable amount, and its own payment method. The POST submit receives indexed `params[:allocations]` (`allocations[N][order_id|include|amount|payment_method]`) and delegates to `Payments::AllocatePayment`. Stimulus controller (`payment_allocation_controller.js`) recomputes the summary live and disables submit if no orders are checked.
* **`payments` table** no longer has the `order_id` column — the relation lives in `payment_allocations`.

### Caja (cash module)

The cash book that replaced the monthly Excel. Two tables, `cash_movements` and `daily_closings`.

* **Six arcas** (`cash_movements.account`, enum from `ACCOUNT_LABELS`): `drawer` (Caja del día) · `main_cash` (Caja grande) · `change_fund` (Remanente) · `bank` · `mercado_pago` · `usd`. **Four reporting groups** (`REPORTING_GROUPS`) collapse them for reporting: `efectivo` = drawer + main_cash + change_fund, `banco`, `mercado_pago`, `usd`. `ACCOUNT_REPORTING_GROUPS` is the inverse lookup, built at load time. **Dollars are never added into a peso total** — every report keeps `usd` apart, by hand, in each query object.
* **No balance is ever stored.** Every figure on every screen is a `SUM(amount)` computed at read time: `CashMovement.balance_for`, `.drawer_balance_on`, `Cash::Reports::HoldingsQuery`. This is the cash equivalent of the `current_stock` rule — there is no column to update, and adding one would be the wrong fix for a slow report.
* **Amounts are signed** (`amount`, `decimal(10,2)`, `numericality: { other_than: 0 }` — zero is never valid). The **model never assigns the sign**; each writer does: `Web::Cash::MovementsController#signed_amount` negates `OUTFLOW_CATEGORIES = %w[suppliers fixed_expense]` and a `partner` row whose submitted `direction` is `withdrawal`; `Cash::RecordTransfer` signs its own two legs; `Cash::ReversePayment` negates the row it mirrors; `Invoices::PayInvoices` passes `-net`; `Cash::CloseDay` stores the discrepancy with whatever sign `counted − expected` has. `Cash::RecordMovement` passes the number through untouched, so **a caller that forgets the sign writes an inflow**.
* **Seven categories** (`CATEGORY_LABELS`): `sale`, `suppliers`, `fixed_expense`, `internal_transfer`, `partner`, `cash_discrepancy`, `opening_balance`. `partner` is the only one that goes both ways, and its direction is **not stored** — only the sign is (`PARTNER_DIRECTION_LABELS` exists for the form, not for the column).
* **What may accompany what** — five conditional validations, all adding to `:base`, and they are bidirectional (each forbids the field as well as requiring it):
  * `account` is **required for every row except a compensation sale**, where it must be blank. Transfer legs, partner rows, discrepancies and opening balances all need one.
  * `channel` is legal **only on `sale`**, and required there. `CHANNEL_LABELS`: `cash, card, qr, transfer, mercado_pago, usd, compensation`; `CHANNEL_ACCOUNTS` is the single place that turns a sale's channel into its arca (`cash→drawer`, `card/qr/transfer→bank`, `mercado_pago→mercado_pago`, `usd→usd`, **`compensation→nil`**).
  * `subcategory` is legal only on, and required for, `fixed_expense` (`SUBCATEGORY_LABELS`: `rent, salaries, social_charges, taxes, utilities, store_expenses`).
  * `supplier` is legal only on, and required for, a **compensation** sale — it names the debt the sale cancels. Adjusting that debt in `invoices` is a manual step outside this module. An invoice payment's outflow does **not** set `supplier`: screens read it through `paid_invoices`.
  * `business_date` may **never be in the future** (same rule on `DailyClosing`). A past day left open is normal and still accepts movements and a close.
* **A compensation sale is always hand-entered.** `PAYMENT_METHOD_CHANNELS` translates `Payment#payment_method` (`cash, bank_qr, bank_card, bank_transfer, mercado_pago`) into a channel and has **no compensation entry**, so `Cash::RecordSaleFromPayment` can never produce one (an unknown method raises `KeyError`, reported as "El método de pago no corresponde a ningún canal de caja"). `compensation` exists nowhere in the app outside this module. **Careful in reports:** a compensation is `category: "sale"` with no arca, so any sum of sales that does not exclude it counts money that never entered a caja.
* **A transfer between arcas is two rows**, both `internal_transfer`, sharing a `transfer_group_id` (uuid) generated once per transfer; the `from` leg carries `−amount` and the `to` leg `+amount`. `Cash::RecordTransfer` is the only writer, it **refuses a non-positive amount** (a negative one is a caller mistake, not a reversal), and its `Result#record` is **the pair, an array** — unlike every other cash service, whose `record` is one row. `Cash::RecordMovement` refuses `category: "internal_transfer"` outright. `CashMovement#transfer?` is `transfer_group_id.present?`; `has_many :transfer_legs` self-joins on that column so `#transfer_counterpart` never re-queries.
* **Closing a day seals it.** `Cash::CloseDay`, in one transaction: reads `expected_cash` = the drawer's balance **before writing anything**; creates the `DailyClosing` (`business_date` has a **unique index** — one closing per day is a DB guarantee); writes a `cash_discrepancy` row if `counted ≠ expected`; sweeps the counted cash `drawer → main_cash` **only if it is positive**; then stamps `daily_closing_id` on every unsealed movement of that date, **one row at a time with `update!`**, never `update_all`.
* **Sealed means frozen.** `#sealed?` is `daily_closing_id.present?`, and `before_update` / `before_destroy` raise `CashMovement::SealedMovementError`. The guard reads **`daily_closing_id_was`** — the persisted value, not the assigned one — which is what lets `CloseDay` stamp a row while blocking every later write to it. It is a Ruby callback, so **`update_all` or raw SQL bypasses it entirely**. A reversal still works on a closed day because `Cash::ReversePayment` never touches the original row, it inserts a new one.
* **A day is never reopened.** There is no route, and `DailyClosingPolicy#update?`/`#destroy?` are hard-coded `false` rather than inherited.
* **Money typed by hand crosses two stages.** `Cash::AmountParser` is the last word on an amount, and it is strict: `PLAIN_DECIMAL = /\A-?\d+(\.\d{1,2})?\z/`, no thousands separator, no comma — anything else returns `nil`. The app's AR display format (`1.500.000,50`) is converted to that shape at the controller boundary by the `CurrencyParser` concern's `decimal_string_from`. Unparseable input is deliberately **passed through unchanged** so the service, not the controller, produces the message. `"101.800"` is the case that motivated the regex: `BigDecimal` reads it as `101.8`.
* **The day screen is one list.** `Cash::DayQuery#entries` returns an **array of `Entry` structs** (not a relation), ordered by `created_at, id`, grouped by `transfer_group_id || id` so a transfer's two legs fold into one entry (outflow leg first). `Entry#kind` is `:in`/`:out`/`:move` and `#counts_in_drawer` is true when any leg is a drawer row — that flag, not a zone, is what the night's count adds up.
* **The day's sales panel** ("Ventas del día", partial `web/cash/days/_sales_by_channel`, id `sales-by-channel`, repainted by every movement/transfer stream) groups the day's sales **by reporting group**, not by channel: `Cash::DayQuery#sales_by_group` folds `sales_by_channel` through `CHANNEL_ACCOUNTS` → `ACCOUNT_REPORTING_GROUPS` (card/qr/transfer land in `banco`). `PESO_SALE_GROUPS` (`efectivo`, `mercado_pago`, `banco`) are always shown, even at 0; `#peso_sales_total` is their sum and `#peso_sales_share` feeds the split bar. **USD and compensation sales are separate rows and never enter the peso total.** "Monto a fajar" (the drawer's net) stays under it.
* **The two "zones" are gone from the screen but alive on the server.** The controller still decides everything from *whether `account` was submitted*: an **arca submission** declares its arca, a **drawer submission** derives one (`"drawer"`, or `CHANNEL_ACCOUNTS[channel]` for a sale). `CashMovementPolicy#categories_for(zone)` allows `sale, suppliers, fixed_expense` in the drawer zone and only `suppliers, fixed_expense` in the arca zone, plus `partner` for an admin; a forged `partner` is refused as a **permission** failure (`forbidden_category?` → `Pundit::NotAuthorizedError`), not a validation one. **Changing what the entry form posts without reading `#zone_category?` and `#account_for` is the easiest way to break this module.**
* **Rows born from a collection or an invoice payment cannot be edited** (their channel/arca is only corrected via `Payments::ChangeMethod`, while the day is open). `#automatic?` is `source_payment_id.present? || paid_invoices.any?` (lists preload `paid_invoices: :supplier` — `DayQuery#entries`, `MovementsQuery#relation` — or it costs a query per row), and `CashMovementPolicy#update?`/`#destroy?` require `!sealed? && !automatic? && !transfer?`. `#reversal?` stays tied to collections (`sale_category? && source_payment_id.present? && outflow?`). On the day list an invoice-payment row reads "Pago <proveedor> — <reference links>" (`CashHelper#cash_paid_invoices_description`), links to the invoice when it paid exactly one (`cash_entry_link`), and shows `—` under *Factura*. Editing and deleting go **straight to the model** (`@movement.update` / `.destroy`, no service); the controller's closed-day check is the first guard and the model callback the second.
* **Where the supplier side writes into caja:** only `Invoices::PayInvoices` (one outflow per payment, see "Invoices"). Outflows for anything that is not a registered invoice (rent, salaries, a bottle of water) stay hand-typed on the day screen.
* **Where the sales side writes into caja** (their channel/arca is only corrected via `Payments::ChangeMethod`, while the day is open): `Payments::CollectSaleNote`, `Payments::CollectOnAccount` and `Payments::AllocatePayment` → `Cash::RecordSaleFromPayment` (idempotent by an application-level `exists?` check on `source_payment_id`, **not** by any DB constraint). `Sales::CancelOrder` → `Cash::ReversePayment`, only for payments the order owns outright. **A reversal is dated `Date.current`, not the original payment's date**, so it can land on a different — even already closed — day than the collection it undoes.
* **Invoicing is per collection, not per sale.** `payments.invoice_type` (`a`/`b`/`none`, **nullable** — `NULL` means nobody has said yet, `none` means it was decided there is no invoice) and `payments.invoice_number` (required for `a`/`b`, forbidden otherwise); `orders` carries neither. One `Payment` is one tender, so a sale collected half in cash and half by Mercado Pago has two invoices to decide, as in the shop's spreadsheet. `Payments::AssignInvoice` is the **only writer**, and **no collection service asks for the invoice**: it is recorded afterwards on the payment page, and stays **editable with the day closed** — it writes `payments`, never a sealed `cash_movements` row. On the day list, *Nota* shows paper numbers only and a *Factura* column shows the collection's invoice (`B · 1452`, `S/F`), a *Facturar* link when unbilled, or `—` for typed rows and for **any row of a reversed collection** (`CashMovement#reversal?`, `Payment#reversal_movement`); every row with a `source_payment` links to its payment page. The close modal lists the date's unbilled collections (`Cash::DayQuery#unbilled_payments`: a positive movement on the date, `invoice_type` NULL, not reversed) with a link each, and **never blocks** the close.
* **Opening balances** are loaded by `lib/tasks/cash.rake` (`cash:opening_balances`), one `opening_balance` row per arca in **one transaction**, refusing to run if any such row already exists.
* **Reports.** `Cash::Reports::HoldingsQuery.new(on:)` → the four holdings at a date (`Row` structs). `Cash::Reports::MonthQuery.new(month:)` → the month's sales as totals plus its fixed expenses **as rows**, newest first, with both fixed totals read off those same rows. `Cash::Reports::MovementsQuery` → a **relation** (the controller paginates it with pagy) plus `#totals`, which sums the whole filter rather than the page. The history's arca filter takes a **reporting group** and matches every fine arca in it, while the table shows the fine arca: filter coarse, read fine, and a filtered history still adds up to the balance it explains. Its *Categoría* filter is the day screen's "Qué es" list (`Web::Cash::ReportsController::CATEGORY_OPTIONS`: each fixed-expense subcategory on its own plus "Gastos fijos (todos)"); a subcategory key filters `fixed_expense` + that subcategory, typed and automatic rows alike. The *Categoría* column names a fixed expense by its subcategory (`cash_category_label`) and the *Proveedor* column also fills from `paid_invoices`. The balance report does not show expenses yet (deferred on purpose). The dashboard's "Caja de \<mes\>" already lists invoice payments of taxes / utilities / social charges, since they are `fixed_expense` rows.

## Key constraints

* **Do not store a cash balance.** Every arca balance is a `SUM(cash_movements.amount)` read at query time; there is no column to update and no total to keep in sync. The twin of the stock rule below.
* **Never `update_all` on `cash_movements`.** The immutability guard that protects a closed day is a Ruby `before_update`/`before_destroy` callback; bulk SQL walks straight past it.
* **Inside `Web::Cash::*` (and `Web::DashboardController`), always write `::Cash::Foo`.** The controllers live in `module Cash` inside `module Web`, so an unqualified `Cash::DayQuery` resolves as `Web::Cash::Cash::DayQuery` and raises `NameError`. Every call site already carries the leading `::`.
* Do **not** bump **`products.current_stock`** ad hoc in controllers/views — keep stock changes through **`StockMovement`** + **`#recalculate_current_stock!`** as the code already does in services.
* **An invoice's `amount` is typed only when it has no lines.** With lines the server computes it and both the create and update paths ignore what the client sends. A blank unit cost is a free line (0); an unparseable one is refused, never read as 0.
* **Stock never gates a sale, a delivery or a cancellation.** The floor is defined once (`Inventory::DeductLineStock`); the restore is the line's net, defined once (`Inventory::RestoreLineStock`). Nothing else computes either.
* **A `Payment` represents a tender** with a single method; its distribution over orders lives in `payment_allocations`. **`Customer#current_balance`** = sum(confirmed credit orders `total_amount`) − sum(`payment_allocations` over those credit orders). Payments allocated to `immediate` sales do not affect the balance.
* **`Customer` validation:** `retail` customers cannot have `has_credit_account: true` (`retail_cannot_have_credit_account`).
* **`Order` validation:** `paper_number` is **required for every order** (unconditional `presence: true`, not unique). Stock is deducted on **every** source, floored at zero — `source` no longer gates stock, and `web/orders/new` always submits `from_paper` (hidden field). It does still gate the amount: `total_amount` must be **`> 0` unless `from_paper?`** and **`>= 0` if `from_paper?`** (`app/models/order.rb`), which is why a UI order may total 0 — and `Sales::ReplaceOrderItem` refuses a swap that leaves the total at zero only when the order is **not** `from_paper`.
* **Manual pricing (`unit_price > 0`):** every order item must have a positive `unit_price` — enforced in **`Sales::CreateOrder`** for all sources, and in Stimulus (`order_form_controller`) by disabling submit while any line price is `<= 0`. Creating an order writes each typed price back inside the transaction: to the channel's own `product_channel_prices` row when the channel has one (Mercado Libre; `price_unit` untouched), otherwise to **`product.price_unit`**. `price_unit` is **not** a protected field like `current_stock`/`cost_unit`, so a direct `update!` is the correct mechanism.
* **Product uniqueness (variants):** the identity of a variant is **`sku (OEM) + product_type + origin + brand`**. The **model validation** (`Product`) enforces uniqueness **only when `origin` is present** (`if: -> { origin.present? }`), to allow progressive loading: the origin is loaded first and the **brand is refined later**; with `origin` at `nil` (importers / raw load) it does not validate. The **DB index** `index_products_on_variant_uniqueness` stays **lenient** (Postgres NULLS DISTINCT → does not block duplicates with `origin`/`brand` at `nil`): it is a **backstop for complete rows**, the **model is the real enforcer**. `sku` = OEM code (repeated across variants); `origin`/`brand` are nullable.
* Not every HTTP action uses a service: e.g. **credit note** CRUD uses **`update` / `save` / `destroy`** on models directly.
* **Timezone-aware dates/times** — always use **`Date.current` / `Time.current`**, never `Date.today` / `Time.now`. The app runs in **`America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires`** (`config.time_zone`, set in `config/application.rb`); timestamps are still **persisted in UTC** (`active_record.default_timezone` is untouched). `Date.today` takes the OS zone and produces an off-by-one near midnight (caused flaky tests in `Invoice`/`Customer`). All code and specs use `Date.current`, which now means the local business day.

## Important gaps

* **`Payment` / `PaymentAllocation`** web UI under `web/customers/:customer_id/payments` (new/create only) uses `Payments::AllocatePayment`; it lets you distribute a collection across several orders with an independent payment method per row.
* **No web UI** for itemized purchasing: **`Purchasing::CreatePurchase`** is used in **seeds/specs**, not `Web::InvoicesController`.
* **`Inventory::SyncFromCsv`** is **rake-only**, not exposed in routes.
* **The cashier cannot reach the cash module.** Every gate in `CashMovementPolicy` and `DailyClosingPolicy` reduces to `user.admin?`, and the three sidebar entries are each rendered behind their own gate (`index?`, `balance_report?`, `movement_history?`). It is deferred on purpose, not cancelled: the screens were built for her (`categories_for(:arca)` already excludes the admin-only `partner`), so re-opening it means changing `index?` and re-reading every method that derives from it.
* **Nothing at the DB level stops a payment being recorded in caja twice.** `Cash::RecordSaleFromPayment` and `Cash::ReversePayment` check with `exists?`/`count` on `source_payment_id`; there is **no unique index**, because a reversal legitimately shares that column with the row it undoes. A concurrent request or a raw `CashMovement.create!` would double-write.
* **Correcting a cash movement does not go through a service.** Creation has a single write path (`Cash::RecordMovement`), but `Web::Cash::MovementsController#update`/`#destroy` call the model directly. The model's validations and the sealed guard are all that protect them.
* **`source` no longer gates stock, but it is not free to drop.** Every sale is on paper (the form always submits `from_paper`) and stock is deducted on every source, yet two live rules still read the column: `Order`'s `total_amount` rule (`> 0` unless `from_paper?`, `>= 0` if `from_paper?`) and `Sales::ReplaceOrderItem`'s guard, which refuses a swap that leaves the total at zero **unless** the order is `from_paper`. The cleanup work-item has to decide what those two become before the column, the scopes and the hidden field can go with a migration.
* **Average cost is not recalculated by invoices with lines** (owner's decision, 2026-09-21): `Product#recalculate_average_cost!` only reads `confirmed` invoices and these are `pending`. The lines keep their `unit_cost`, so it can be computed later.

## Notes

* **System specs always run at 1400×900.** `spec/rails_helper.rb` registers `:selenium_chrome_headless` itself (remote Selenium when `SELENIUM_REMOTE_URL` is set, as in Docker; local Chrome otherwise, as in CI) with `--window-size=1400,900`, because Rails ignores `driven_by`'s `screen_size` for drivers it does not register — without it CI ran at Chrome's default 800×600 and the cash day table hid its last column.
* **`db/seeds.rb` builds an open cash day for `Date.current`** through the real collection, transfer and movement services (a replay of a real day: ventas 1.127.300, a fajar 23.000), invoiced like the spreadsheet with notes 4411 and 4429 left unbilled. Seeded sale notes are 80% under 100.000 and every seeded amount is a multiple of 100. It also seeds AFIP (bills taxes + social charges: IVA, Ganancias and SICOSS boletas for the previous month) and Edesur (utilities), and unlinks invoices from cash movements before wiping `cash_movements` (the `invoices.cash_movement_id` FK).
* For behavior, **read the service (or controller action) in question** — “thin controller” is the norm for orders, but invoices/credit notes mix services and direct updates.
